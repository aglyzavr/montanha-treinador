## Long-Term Logic & 3-Year Arc

Three years is enough time to do this right; it is not enough time to take shortcuts. Each year develops one core capacity that the next year builds upon. Skip a year and you arrive at UTMB with a critical gap that breaks you at km 130.

**Year 1 (2026-27):** Fix the gaps. Strength, foot durability, threshold work. Race shorter to allow the foundation to be built.

**Year 2 (2027-28):** Build the volume and vert tolerance. First properly-trained 100K. Stones accumulation begins in earnest.

**Year 3 (2028-29):** UTMB-specific preparation. 100M qualifier. Lottery entry.

## Foot Durability Protocol

Parallel workstream throughout the cycle. **The primary success/failure variable for Year 2-3.**

### Lessons from Pirineu 2025 + OMD 2026 (combined diagnosis)

Two separate but compounding failure modes:

1. **Geometry/gear interaction:** Recurring lateral heel callus and blisters in same location both races. Likely heel cup mismatch with Nnormal last, possibly amplified by sock seam.

## Long-Race Playbook (hypothermia, fueling, crew)

### Hypothermia Mitigation (Pirineu km 91 lesson)

In any race >12 hours, especially in mountains or cool/wet conditions:

- Jacket on at first sign of cooling, BEFORE shivering starts
- Warm liquids at aid stations in cold conditions (broth, tea)
- Don't let calorie deficit exceed 2,000 kcal
- Carry emergency layer (light wind/rain shell) at all times
- HR drop + chills + glycogen depletion is a dangerous combination - treat early

### Fueling Standards (long races)

**Per hour target:** ~250 kcal
- 1-2 gels/hour (60-90g carbs)
- Real food at aid stations: bread, banana, watermelon, salty foods, soup
- 500ml water/hour minimum (more in heat)
- 500-700mg sodium/hour
- Start fueling at hour 1, every 30 min after

**Pirineu nutrition learnings:** Melon worked surprisingly well. Pasta with tuna at km 60 successful. Continue using real food strategy alongside gels.

Zhanna's Pirineu crewing approach was excellent and worth building into future plans:
- Multi-aid-station support pattern
- Calm, decision-preserving tone at crisis points ("run one more segment, I'll come get you if it gets worse")
- Logistics: drive in mountain terrain, SMS via friend tracking, food preparation

Conversation point for late 2026: confirm Zhanna's availability and willingness for 2027 100K crew support. Long-term: full crew + drop bags + aid station plan for 2028 100M qualifier and 2029 UTMB 100M.
